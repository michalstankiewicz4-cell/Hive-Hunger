Hive Hunger — a 3D swarm game

Hive Hunger is a free 3D browser game about a hungry swarm. The swarm flies like a flock of birds, finds the nearest matter on its own and eats a voxel planet crater by crater. Click or tap to steer it to a new spot.

Adjust the swarm live with sliders: number of units, speed, bite power, spacing and cohesion. When a planet is eaten, the swarm flies home and a new planet appears.

Play alone or with up to four players in your browser: create a Co-op or PvP room and share the link — no account or download needed.
