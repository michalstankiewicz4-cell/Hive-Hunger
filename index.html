Hive Hunger — a 3D swarm game

Hive Hunger is a free 3D browser game about a hungry swarm. The swarm flies like a flock of birds, finds the nearest matter on its own and eats a voxel planet crater by crater. Click or tap to steer it to a new spot.

Missions on a map of the solar system go from the Kuiper Belt — a band of small icy asteroids — past Pluto, the giants and their moons, Mars and Earth, in to the Sun.

Mix 13 ship types: eaters, lasers, storm ships, plasma orbs, liquid splashers, bombers that leave black holes, drills, piercers, snipers, tugs, support ships, cleaners and boosters with pink force fields. Spend points on upgrades, tune the swarm live with sliders, and save your game.

Play alone or with up to four players in your browser: create a Co-op or PvP room and share the link — no account or download needed.
